## Fotografia automotiva

Arquivo: `public/images/gtblock-hero.webp` (original em `public/images/gtblock-hero.png`).

Criada com a ferramenta integrada ImageGen em 24/10/2026. Imagem conceitual para comunicação visual, sem representar um veículo de cliente ou recuperação real.

Prompt utilizado:

> Create a premium automotive advertising photograph as a website hero background. Wide landscape 16:9 composition. A modern graphite black luxury sports SUV, generic unbranded, seen from front three-quarter angle facing toward the left, positioned in the RIGHT HALF of the frame, entire vehicle visible with large wheels and sculpted body. Camera low near ground. Dramatic single warm champagne gold light strip rim lighting on the roof and hood, white LED headlights. Nearly black architectural studio, subtle smoky dark background, glossy dark asphalt with restrained gold reflection. The LEFT 45 PERCENT must be very dark empty negative space for white website headline overlaid later. Sophisticated editorial automotive campaign, realistic proportions, beautiful detailed photographic rendering, restrained gold lighting, high contrast. No text, no logos, no badges, no UI, no watermark. Image will be displayed full-bleed on a black and gold vehicle tracking brand website.
